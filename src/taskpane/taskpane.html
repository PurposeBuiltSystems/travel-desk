Add your travelers

Type or paste their email addresses — a pasted Outlook To: line works. Travel Desk writes one invitation for you to review and send; each person then opens the add-in, clicks Find my setup, and they're configured. Nothing to copy or paste.

Nothing secret is in the code: the planner link, coordinator and finance addresses, and your fiscal-year settings.
