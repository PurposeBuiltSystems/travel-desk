Travel Authorization Form

Template · every label here is one Travel Desk already knows.

Why this exists. Travel Desk reads a completed authorization form and fills a request from it — but only as well as the form is labelled. Forms whose fields are named FillText1 and Text5 cannot be read by anything. Every label below matches Travel Desk's own, so a form filled out on this template is read in full with nothing to configure.

Fill it in here and Print → Save as PDF, or print it blank and complete it by hand. Attach the PDF to an email, open Travel Desk, and click Fill from this email.
